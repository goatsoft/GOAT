## What this covers

The "Brand Assets": the GOAT name, the horned-goat mark and app icons (all variants, including the 1337 and professional icon sets), the Goatie mascot and every pose and animation of it, the theme artwork (Light, Pasture, Midnight, System and 1337 scenes, teasers and previews), the spinner and loading indicators, speech bubbles, illustrations, the website hero and background art, and any source files, exports, sprite sheets or renders of the above, wherever they live in this repository. Today that includes, without limitation:

- `apps/goat-macos/art/` (source masters)
- `apps/goat-macos/App/Assets.xcassets/` (app icons, `goat-*`, `goatie-*`, `glook*`, `goat-poop` and other image sets)
- `assets/`
- `web/public/img/`, GOAT favicon and touch-icon exports in `web/public/`, and GOAT image assets under `web/src/`

Colour tokens, typography choices and layout values in code and in [docs/DESIGN.md](docs/DESIGN.md) are part of the software and remain MIT; the pictures are not. Third-party assets, including website icons and the Hindsight integration mark, are excluded from these Brand Assets and keep their own licences, listed in [THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md](THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md).

## What you may do

- Build and run GOAT from this repository for your own use, including the Brand Assets as they are bundled.
- Redistribute the Brand Assets **only as part of unmodified official GOAT builds and this repository**, so that a checkout, a fork used for contributing, or a mirror of an official release keeps working.
- Use the GOAT name and mark to refer to GOAT (reviews, articles, links, "works with GOAT"), and show screenshots of the app.

## What you may not do

- Ship a modified, renamed or rebranded build of GOAT that still carries the Brand Assets. A fork you distribute must replace the icons, mascot and theme artwork with your own and must not use the GOAT name or mark in a way that suggests it is GOAT or endorsed by it.
- Use the Brand Assets in other software, products, merchandise, themes, extensions or marketing.
- Extract, adapt, trace or train on the artwork, or publish the source masters, outside the redistribution allowed above.

## Community themes and extensions

The GOAT Theme Format and the extension API are MIT. Themes and extensions you make are yours; they may say they are "for GOAT" but may not reuse the Brand Assets.
