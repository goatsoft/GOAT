# GOAT community code of conduct

GOAT welcomes people who want to use, improve and maintain the project. Participation should be respectful and accessible across backgrounds, identities, experience levels and abilities. These expectations apply to project spaces and to people representing GOAT elsewhere.

## Working together

Discuss ideas and technical decisions on their merits. Explain disagreements clearly, listen to relevant evidence and leave room for people to learn. Give feedback that helps someone improve their contribution. Respect maintainers’ and contributors’ time; open-source participation does not create an obligation to provide immediate support.

Harassment, intimidation, discriminatory abuse, sexual harassment and threats are unacceptable. Do not expose someone’s personal information, repeatedly contact them after they have asked you to stop, or use project spaces to target an individual. Disagreement is welcome; personal attacks are not.

## Reporting a concern

Report concerns privately to [conduct@goatapp.dev](mailto:conduct@goatapp.dev). Include the relevant messages or links, what happened and any immediate concern about continued contact. Share only the personal information needed to understand the report.

GitHub’s security vulnerability form is not a conduct-reporting channel. Do not publish another person’s private information in an issue while seeking help.

## How maintainers respond

Maintainers will assess reports with discretion and consider the conduct, its impact and any previous incidents. They may remove material, ask for a change in behaviour, limit participation or exclude someone from project spaces. Serious threats or repeated harassment may require immediate restrictions.

Information from a report will be shared only as needed to investigate and respond. The reporter and the person affected by a moderation decision may provide additional context through the private contact. A maintainer directly involved in a complaint should not be its sole reviewer.

These rules are intended to support a useful, professional community for users and contributors. They apply to maintainers as well as everyone else.
